How to play APMed'dle

  • Guess today's term in 6 tries. Each guess is a real word (English or from our courses) with the same number of letters.
  • After each guess the tiles show how close you are:
AIn the word, right spot
AIn the word, wrong spot (dashed)
ANot in the word
  • After 3 guesses the topic appears. After 4 you can show the clue (your result gets a 💡).
  • One word a day for the whole class, from the subject with the nearest eval. A new word comes at midnight.
