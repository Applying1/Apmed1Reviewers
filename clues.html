Three Clues

Name today's mystery term in as few clues as you can.

    How to play Three Clues

    • One mystery term a day for the whole class, from the subject with the nearest eval.
    • You start with the hardest clue. Name the term now for 3 points.
    • A wrong answer, or asking for the next clue, opens an easier one: 2 points, then 1.
    • You get one answer per clue. Type a few letters and pick from the list, so spelling never costs you.
    • Afterward you see why the clues point to that term. A new one comes at midnight.
