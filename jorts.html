Mechanics & Guidelines 🩳

  • Open to all students who want to join the jorts agenda. Male and female are not separated—one unified battle!
  • Dress Code: Participants must come wearing their best jorts fit. Any style, any wash, any level of questionable fashion decisions.
  • Styling is completely up to you: Accessories, layering, double-belts, waist chains, and other creative additions are highly welcome.
  • Runway Moments: Each participant will get their moment to walk the runway, pose, and show off their fit.
  • Audience Voting: Everyone gets to vote for their favorite fit. Votes will be based on overall look, creativity, styling, and confidence.
  • Reminders: No need to buy new jorts! Work with what you have! DIYs and customized raw-hem jorts are highly encouraged.
