Official Mechanics

  • Open to all students: Join the jorts agenda! Male and female compete together in one unified catwalk.
  • Dress Code: Participants must wear their best jorts fit. Any style, any wash, any level of questionable fashion decisions.
  • Styling is up to you: Layering, statement belts, chains, shoes, and creative accessories are welcome.
  • Runway Walk: Each participant gets their moment to walk, strike signature poses, and show off their fit.
  • Voting: Everyone gets to vote based on overall look, creativity, styling, and confidence.
  • No need to buy new: Work with what you have. DIY cutoffs and frayed hems are highly celebrated!
