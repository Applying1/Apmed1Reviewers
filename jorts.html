Official Mechanics

01

OPEN TO ALL

Join the jorts agenda! Male and female compete together in one unified catwalk.

02

DRESS CODE

Participants must wear their best jorts fit. Any style, any wash, any level of questionable fashion decisions.

03

STYLING

Layering, statement belts, chains, shoes, and creative accessories are fully welcomed and encouraged.

04

RUNWAY WALK

Each participant gets their moment to walk, strike signature poses, and show off their fit on stage.

05

VOTING

Audience votes based on overall look, creativity, styling execution, and stage confidence.

06

DIY CUTOFFS

No need to buy new! Work with what you have. DIY cutoffs, raw edges, and frayed hems are highly celebrated.
