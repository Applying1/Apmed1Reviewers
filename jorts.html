Official Event Mechanics

  • Open to all students: Join the jorts agenda! Male and female are competing together in one unified catwalk showcase.
  • Dress Code: Participants must arrive wearing their best jorts fit. Any style, any wash, any level of questionable fashion decisions.
  • Styling is up to you: Layering, statement belts, chains, footwear, and creative accessories are strongly encouraged.
  • Runway Walk: Every contestant gets their moment to walk, strike signature poses, and show off their fit.
  • Audience Voting: Everyone gets to vote. Top 5 overall advance from the audience vote to the Final Runway round.
  • No need to buy new: Work with what you have. DIY cutoffs and frayed hems are highly celebrated!
