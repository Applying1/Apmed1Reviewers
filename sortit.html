How to play Sort It

  • Put every term in its group: tap a term, then tap a group. You can also drag it.
  • Changed your mind? Tap a term in a group to pick it up again.
  • When the tray is empty, tap Check. Right ones lock in ✓; wrong ones jump back to the tray.
  • Sort everything in one check for a perfect day. Afterward you see why each term belongs where it does.
  • One set a day for the whole class, from the subject with the nearest eval. A new set comes at midnight.
