Take attendance

Each day starts from the tracker. Go table by table and tap anyone who isn't there: each tap changes Present → Absent → Late → Excused. A dashed outline means the sheet doesn't have that change yet; Save to tracker writes it in.

Send to the tracker

“Save to tracker” writes your changes (the dashed ones) straight into this day's column of the tracker. If that doesn't work, “Copy for the sheet” copies 50 marks in roll order (1 to 50). In the tracker, click this day's cell on roll 1's row and paste: each mark lands in its own row. The site picks it up on the next hourly update (or tap Update attendance now on the Moderate page).
