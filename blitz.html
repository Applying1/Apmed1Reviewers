How Blitz works

  • Pick a course. You get 60 seconds of prompts from its matching sets and flashcards.
  • Tap the term that fits. Each right answer scores 10 points.
  • Every 5 in a row raises the multiplier (up to ×4) and adds 2 seconds.
  • A wrong tap shows the right answer, pauses the clock for a moment and resets your run.
  • Play as often as you like. Your best score per course is kept.
