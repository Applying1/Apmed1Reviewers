Privacy and Terms

Last updated October 19, 2026

APMed '33 Reviewers is a free study site made by and for students of APMed batch 2033. It is not an official school website. This page explains, in plain words, what the site stores and the few rules for using it.

Privacy

What stays on your device

Your progress, answers, notes settings, colors and name are saved in your browser on your own device. They are not sent anywhere unless you turn on one of the options below.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you do, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and we store your study progress so it can follow you across devices. We do not get your password and cannot see anything else in your Google account.

Sync codes

If you use a sync code instead, your study progress is stored under that code. Anyone with the code can load that progress, so keep it to yourself.

Class stats

When class stats are on, the site sends anonymous right and wrong counts per question, so everyone can see which topics the class finds hardest. These counts are not linked to your name or account.

Questions and reports you send

If you suggest a question or report a problem, the text you write is stored so a student moderator can review it. Approved questions are shown to everyone in that course. Don't put personal information in them.

Where it's kept and who can see it

Online data is stored with Supabase, a database service. Only the student who runs this site can view it, and only to keep the site working. We never sell or share your data, and we don't use ads or trackers. The site uses Google Fonts, which loads fonts from Google's servers.

Deleting your data

You can clear everything on your device in a course's Personalize page, or by clearing your browser data. To delete your online progress or sign-in information, message the site owner in the APMed '33 group chat and it will be removed.

Terms of use

  • For studying only. The reviewers are student-made study aids. Always check against your teachers' slides and books; if they disagree, your teachers are right.
  • No guarantees. The site is provided free, as is. It may have mistakes, change, or be unavailable at times.
  • Be respectful. Don't submit offensive, copied or misleading content, and don't try to break or overload the site.
  • Course materials such as transes are shared for batch members' personal study. Please don't repost them publicly.

Questions? Ask in the APMed '33 group chat.
