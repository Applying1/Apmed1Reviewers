    How to write questions

    Leave a blank line between questions. Give the right answer on an A: line and wrong ones on X: lines, or number the question and mark the right choice with *. Up to 5 choices. A question with only an A: line (no wrong choices) is identification: it is answered by typing.

    # Topic name (optional)
Q: Who is called the father of medicine?
A: Hippocrates
X: Galen
X: Imhotep
Why: An explanation (optional)
Hint: A hint (optional)

1. Which river runs through Egypt?
a. Tigris
*b. Nile
c. Euphrates

    CSV or Google Sheets: one question per row: question, answer, then the wrong answers. A header row can name the columns question, answer, wrong, why, hint and topic. You can also paste the cells straight from the sheet.

    A topic that starts with a section number of this reviewer, like 1-1, puts its questions into that section.
