How to play

  • Tap a square and type. Tap the same square again to switch between Across and Down.
  • Tap a clue, or use the arrows on the clue bar, to jump between words.
  • Stuck? Hint on the clue bar shows one letter of the word at a time (like A _ _ _ G _ _). The Hints menu can also check or reveal. Each counts as a hint.
  • Signed in? Your puzzle follows you to your other devices, and your time goes on the class board (time + 15 s per hint). You can hide yourself from it anytime.
  • A new puzzle comes out every day at midnight, mixing the courses with the nearest evals or exams. The small tag on each clue shows its course. Your answers save on this device.
