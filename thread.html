Common Thread

Find the four groups of four.

How to play Common Thread

  • Sixteen terms hide four groups of four. The group names are hidden too.
  • Tap four terms that share a thread, then tap Submit.
  • Right: the group is revealed. Wrong: you lose one of your four mistakes. You are told when you are one term away.
  • Watch for terms that seem to fit two groups. Only one way sorts all sixteen.
  • One grid a day for the whole class, from the subject with the nearest eval. A new grid comes at midnight.
